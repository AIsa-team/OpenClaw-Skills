# Generative Engine Optimization: How to Get Cited by AI Search

Search is changing. More and more people get their answers from AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity instead of clicking ten blue links, and marketing teams are scrambling to keep up. In this guide we walk through what we have learned about showing up in those answers, why it matters for traffic, and what you can do about it this quarter.

## Why AI answers matter now

AI Overviews now appear on roughly 30% of Google queries, and click-through rates on those queries have dropped by about 35%. Some publishers report that traffic from Google fell by half within a year. At the same time, referral traffic from ChatGPT and Perplexity grew more than 10x in 2025.

The upshot is simple: if an answer engine summarises your topic without citing you, you are invisible.

## How answer engines choose sources

Answer engines still start from a retrieval step. Google's AI features pull from the regular index, so pages that are not indexed or that are blocked from snippets cannot be cited. ChatGPT with browsing and Perplexity run web searches and then pick a handful of pages to quote.

From what we have seen, three things make a page easy to quote:

- A clear, direct statement of the answer near the top of the page.
- Sections with descriptive headings that map to the questions people actually ask.
- Specific facts, numbers and examples that an engine can lift verbatim.

### Structure beats length

Long articles are not automatically better. Engines extract passages, so a 900 word page with tight sections can outperform a 4,000 word page that buries the answer.

### Original data wins

Pages with original research, benchmarks, or first-hand testing are cited far more often than summaries of other people's work. When we published our own benchmark of five prediction market APIs, it was picked up by Perplexity within two weeks.

## What to do this quarter

1. Add a two sentence answer directly under the H1 of every key page.
2. Rewrite section headings as the questions your customers ask.
3. Publish at least one piece of original data per month.
4. Check that your pages are indexable and not blocked with nosnippet.
5. Track which of your pages are cited in AI answers.

## Conclusion

Generative engine optimization is not a trick. It rewards the same things good content always has: clarity, structure, and evidence. Start with your top pages and measure what changes.
